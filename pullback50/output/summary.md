# 50-day undercut & reclaim: backtest summary

Data: 2015-01-02 to 2026-10-27, today's S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100 members (survivorship bias: stocks that left the indexes are missing). Independent trades, each sized to risk 1% of $100k (max 25% of the account per position; no-stop version uses a fixed 12.5%). 0.1% slippage per side.

Every signal is taken (one position per stock), so many trades overlap: "Max open" shows the most held at once. Drawdown is therefore shown in R (the worst peak-to-trough run of cumulative R across trades in exit order); the dollar figure in summary.csv adds up all overlapping trades and is not a realistic account drawdown. "Stopped→24%" = share of stopped-out trades whose price still reached the +24% target within 60 trading days of entry. Trades still open at the end of the data are marked to the last close.
